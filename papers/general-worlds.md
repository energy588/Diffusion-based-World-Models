
# General-purpose Worlds

General-purpose diffusion world models target open-ended video generation, digital twins, games, and scalable 3D/4D simulation.

## Key Directions
- Long-form video world generation
- Interactive game and environment simulation
- Digital twins and world foundation models
- 3D / 4D physical scene evolution
- Controllable multimodal world generation

## Representative Topics
- Sora-style large-scale video world models
- Game and environment simulators
- Long-horizon video generation systems
- 3D / 4D diffusion world models
