
# Embodied Intelligence

In embodied intelligence, diffusion-based world models act as imagination engines for robots and agents.

## Key Directions
- Action-conditioned visual prediction
- Robot manipulation and foresight
- Vision-language-action (VLA) + world models
- Policy learning from imagined rollouts
- Sim-to-real transfer and interactive control

## Representative Topics
- Manipulation world models
- Embodied video prediction
- Interactive simulators for policy learning
- VLA-enhanced diffusion world models
